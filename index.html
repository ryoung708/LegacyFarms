Our story

“Tomorrow's Harvest, Grown Locally Today”

We wanted to grow something healthy for our own family at home. That is how Legacy Farms started.

I’m a diesel technician by trade, so I’ve always built my career around complex mechanical systems, precision calibration, and absolute attention to detail. When we decided to build an indoor facility to cultivate clean microgreens, applying that same technical, hands-on mindset to managing our grow trays and environment just felt natural.

We built our farm around the idea that food should be both nourishing and local. We wanted a simple, dependable way to bring fresh microgreens to families nearby without any of the hassle or guesswork.

Every single part of our process is kept honest and straightforward: precision indoor growing, small-batch quality, and a dependable Saturday porch drop that you can count on every single week.

Rooted in McCordsville When we are not managing the farm or checking on trays, we are living and working right here alongside our family, neighbors, and community that makes this place so special to stay rooted.

That is our simple mission: grow fresh, small-batch microgreens right here in town and make it easy for local families to add something healthy to their everyday meals.
