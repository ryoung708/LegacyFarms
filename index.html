Our story

“Tomorrow's Harvest, Grown Locally Today”

We wanted to grow something healthy for our own family at home. That is how Legacy Farms started.

We built our farm around the idea that food should be both nourishing and local. We wanted a simple, dependable way to bring fresh microgreens to families nearby.

From growing systems to customer experience, every part of Legacy Farms is designed to keep it real: good food, local sourcing, and a dependable Saturday porch drop.

Rooted in McCordsville When we are not managing the farm or growing trays, we are working with the family and community that makes this place special.

That is the mission: grow small-batch, fresh microgreens and make it easy for local families to add something healthy to their everyday meals.
