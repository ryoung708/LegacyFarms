How Our Neighborhood System Works

Step 1: Secure Your Harvest Box

Click the sign-up button above to claim your neighborhood slot. We grow exclusively to order each week to ensure zero food waste and perfect freshness.

Step 2: Bi-Weekly Porch Delivery

Every other Friday morning, your fresh greens are harvested, packed, and dropped cleanly straight onto your Woodhaven porch.

Step 3: Simple Mobile Pay

No complex recurring credit card sign-ups. You simply send your payment via Venmo or Cash App on delivery morning after your box arrives.
