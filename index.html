⚡ Quick Ways to Eat Your Greens

🥤 The 5-Second Smoothie Boost

Toss a heavy handful of Sweet Pea shoots directly into your morning fruit blender. It adds a subtle sweet note and completely vanishes inside the blend while packing a massive antioxidant punch.

🥪 The Premium Sandwich Crunch

Ditch soggy iceberg lettuce. Layer a thick bedding of thick, nutty Sunflower shoots inside your wraps, burgers, or turkey sandwiches for an unbeatably fresh crisp that won't wilt.

🥗 The Ultimate 2-Minute Base Salad

Mix equal parts Sunflower and Pea shoots into a bowl, toss lightly with cherry tomatoes, a splash of olive oil, and simple balsamic dressing for a nutrient-dense gourmet side salad.

🍕 Dinner & Pizza Toppers

Right before serving hot dinners, throw a raw crop over steak, tacos, or hot take-out pizza. The ambient heat gently softens the stems without soaking away vital live enzymes.
