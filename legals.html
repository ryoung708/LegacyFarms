Refunds & Cancellations

Because our products are perishable and grown to order, cancellations should be requested as early as possible. Orders placed for Saturday delivery must be changed or cancelled before **Tuesday 9:00 PM** local time to be included in that week’s harvest planning.

If an order is missing, damaged, or spoiled upon delivery, contact us at legacyfarms.mccordsville@proton.me and we will evaluate and work to make it right (refund or replacement at our discretion).

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Deliveries are scheduled on Saturdays within our stated delivery zone. We perform porch drop (no contact). Customers are strictly required to leave a clean, insulated cooler or thermal bag out on their porch prior to delivery. Legacy Farms is not responsible for the spoilage, degradation, loss, or theft of products after they are delivered to the stated delivery location or left exposed to weather elements due to a missing cooler.

Estimated delivery windows and zone coverage are posted on the site; sign up for the waitlist if your neighborhood is not yet served.
